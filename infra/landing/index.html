Vision

Strategy that respects your time.

Map-first

The hex board is the game. Biomes shape economy, terrain shapes battle, exploration unlocks the rest. No bloated UI — the map tells you what to do next.

Mobile-native

One-thumb friendly. Pinch the map, tap a tile, queue a city, end your turn on the bus. Built mobile-first — desktop just gets a bigger window.

Browser-instant

No installs, no store, no patch days. Open the URL, the game loads in a couple seconds, your save lives in the cloud, your friends can join the same link.

Status

What's already built — and what's next.

  • M1–M16

    Deterministic headless 4X simulation, faction stats, M1 skirmish combat, biome economy, save/load — all stress-tested in the original Unity prototype.

  • v0.1 web

    Porting the engine to TypeScript, building a custom hex renderer + responsive HUD. Single-player vs AI, map of 12×8 tiles, three unit types, five-tech tree, domination win.

  • v0.2

    Full tech tree, diplomacy drift, scout contact rumors, faction synergies, persistent profiles.

  • v0.3+

    Async PvP (yesterday's winners become tomorrow's AI), USDTg-funded weekly leagues, player-made map seeds.
